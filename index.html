The Fishito Heritage

Pristine Ocean Sourcing,
Refined For Global Palates.

Centuries of maritime tradition along Sri Lanka's southern and eastern shores meet cutting-edge food safety protocols. At FISHITO, we partner directly with licensed artisanal fishing fleets to ensure fish are processed within hours of catch.

Direct Coastal Boat Catch

Sourced from small day-boats operating in clean Sri Lankan waters, guaranteeing maximum freshness before curing begins.

Elevated Raised Mesh Racks

Unlike traditional ground drying, all Fishito seafood cures on elevated solar mesh beds inside ventilated drying structures, 100% free from sand and pests.

Export Grade Packaging Options

Custom branded retail stand-up zip pouches (100g, 150g, 200g) or heavy-duty food-grade master cartons (5kg, 10kg) for bulk food distributors.

Five-Stage Processing

The Art & Science of Pure Solar Curing

Every batch undergoes a calibrated five-stage sequence to retain rich natural omega fatty acids, authentic umami depth, and pristine food safety.

01

Dawn Catch

Sourced immediately from return boats at sunrise to eliminate deterioration.

02

Hand-Sorting & Wash

Thoroughly rinsed in purified sea water and hand-inspected for size and texture.

03

Raised Rack Curing

Evenly spread on elevated food-grade mesh racks under equatorial sunlight.

04

Moisture Calibration

Electronic moisture metering verifies consistent water activity below safety thresholds.

05

Barrier Packaging

Vacuum-sealed into oxygen-barrier film ready for sea and air freight.
